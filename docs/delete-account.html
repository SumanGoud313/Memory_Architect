Memory Architect

Delete your account

Two ways to permanently delete your Memory Architect account and data — from inside the app (instant), or by request if you no longer have access to it.

01

Delete from inside the app

This is the fastest way — it takes effect immediately, no waiting.

  1. Open Memory Architect and sign in if you aren't already.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account and tap it.
  4. Confirm in the dialog that appears. This can't be undone.
This is permanent. Once confirmed, your account and everything tied to it are deleted right away — there's no recovery window.
02

What gets deleted

  • Your account and its Google sign-in link
  • XP, coins, streaks, and achievements
  • Mission, Lucky Spin, and Mystery Chest progress
  • Owned and equipped cosmetics, and your consumables inventory
  • Your current Daily, Weekly, and Monthly leaderboard entries
  • All data cached locally on your device
One narrow exception: leaderboard periods that already closed before you deleted your account (a past day/week/month you competed in) may retain an anonymized historical entry — the same way a completed sports season's standings aren't rewritten after the fact. It's no longer linked to your account, sign-in, or any personal identifier.
03

Can't access the app anymore?

If you've uninstalled the app, lost access to your Google account, or otherwise can't reach Settings → Delete Account, email a deletion request and it'll be handled manually.

Email your request from the Google account you used to sign in, or include the display name shown on your Profile/leaderboards so the account can be identified:
