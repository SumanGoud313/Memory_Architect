## How these were made

`tools/audio/synth_sfx.js` (committed to this repo) computes raw 16-bit PCM samples directly from
sine-wave oscillators shaped by hand-written attack/decay envelopes — a rising pitch-swept "pop"
for pickup, a two-click "ratchet" tick for rotate, and a low body tone plus a fast contact
transient for place — then writes each as a standard WAV file. Three pitch-varied takes per sound
exist so `GameAudioManagerImpl`'s new no-consecutive-repeat picker (`VariantPicker`) never plays
the exact same clip twice in a row. Nothing in the script reads, samples, or references any
existing audio file, recording, or third-party library. It is deterministic and re-runnable:
`node tools/audio/synth_sfx.js app/src/main/assets/audio/sfx` regenerates byte-identical output.
